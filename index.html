1. Home

Your Home screen (mobile)

Home is the daily desk. Same data in every Experience Mode — only how much you see changes. Tap PX in the bottom bar to try Beginner, Standard, or Power User. No data loss.

PX Beg

Beginner

Zero dashboard. Greeting, today’s health, a task list. Tap a row to log with Buddy.

Bottom tabs: Home · Foodiary · Progress · Menu

  1. 1
    Top bar

    kCal Buddy, language, theme, and Settings.

  2. 2
    Greeting

    Buddy says hello. One line. That’s the whole coach.

  3. 3
    Today’s Health

    Calories, protein, fiber, water, drinks, and your burn vs goal. Tap a row for details.

  4. 4
    Daily task list

    Breakfast · Lunch · Dinner · Snack · Drinks · Water. Tap a box → Buddy. It checks off only after you save.

  5. 5
    Bottom tabs

PX Std

Standard

Buddy is the host. Today’s Progress is the compass. Health cards sit under the budget.

Bottom tabs: Home · Meal Plan · Foodiary · Activity · Progress · Spaces · Menu

  1. 1Buddy host
  2. 2Toolbox
  3. 3More
  4. 4Today clock
  5. 5Today’s Progress
  6. 6Health cards
  7. 7Bottom tabs
  1. 1
    Buddy host

    Larger Buddy and a short greeting. Language, theme, and Settings stay on the right.

  2. 2
    Toolbox

    Camera is the orange hero. Water, Weight, and Cart stay quiet (white chips, colored icons). Cart is last. Dashboard cards keep the strong colors.

  3. 3
    More

    Camera’s chevron: Camera, Photo, Receipt / File, Text input, Voice — A–Z. Text is how you type a meal.

  4. 4
    Today clock

    Date, time, sun timeline, weather. Context — not a log.

  5. 5
    Today’s Progress

    Daily energy budget: Food · Exercise · Remaining. No TEF in Standard.

  6. 6
    Health cards

    Protein · Fiber · Water · Drinks. Tap a card for details.

  7. 7
    Bottom tabs

    Home plus Meal Plan, Foodiary, Activity, Progress, Spaces, PX Std, Menu.

PX Pwr

Power User

Same Home as Standard, with TEF and leucine on the budget and protein card.

Bottom tabs: Same tabs as Standard · desktop can add Reports · Insights · Goals

  1. 1Buddy host
  2. 2Energy budget + TEF
  3. 3Protein + leucine
  4. 4Everything else
  1. 1
    Buddy host

    Same toolbox as Standard: Camera · Water · Weight · Cart.

  2. 2
    Energy budget + TEF

    Food · TEF · Exercise · Remaining. TEF is the extra burn from eating — not a second meal.

  3. 3
    Protein + leucine

    Protein card shows a leucine estimate. Fiber, water, drinks stay as in Standard.

  4. 4
    Everything else

    Clock, tip, mission, Spaces glance — richer detail, same jobs. PX Pwr in the bottom bar.

PX is beta presentation only. Your logs stay. Switch anytime.
