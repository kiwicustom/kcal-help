How to join the beta

On Android, install the app first. Then request access or open the invite inside kCal Buddy.

Highly recommended: install the Android app as step 1 — tap Download Android Beta.

Download Android Beta

GitHub serves the APK — no kcal.lol login. Android may ask to allow installs from this browser. That is expected.

  1. 1

    Tap Download Android Beta above. Install kCal Buddy, then open the orange app.

  2. 2

    In the app, tap Request access — or open the invite email if Sam already sent one.

  3. 3

    Use the same email. Create a PIN — that is your account.

There is no public signup. One invite email = one account. Do not share the invite link.

Request access on kcal.lol

iPhone · invitation only

How to join the beta

On iPhone the app comes through TestFlight first. There is no APK and no App Store listing yet.

  1. 1

    Open kcal.lol — tap Request access.

  2. 2

    Type your name, then the email we should use for the kcal invite.

  3. 3

    Send Sam your Apple Account email (the one you use for the App Store) so he can add you in TestFlight.

  4. 4

    Accept Apple’s TestFlight invitation, then install kCal Buddy from TestFlight.

  5. 5

    Open the kcal invite with the same email. Create a PIN — that is your account.

Open the iPhone TestFlight steps

There is no public signup. One invite email = one account. Do not share the invite link.

Your Home screen (mobile)

Home is the daily desk. Same data in every Experience Mode — only how much you see changes. Tap the ? in the top bar to label Home in waves. On-screen Click here to CONTINUE / Click here to go BACK, and Click here to CLOSE on the last wave — nothing auto-advances. Tap PX in the bottom bar to try Beginner, Standard, or Power User. No data loss.
