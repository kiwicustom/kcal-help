How kcal works

One product. One loop. Log with Buddy on Home → it lands in Foodiary → Today's Progress updates.

💬 Buddy Tip

Prefer the big orange Log on Home (text / photo / voice). Open Foodiary to fix grams, times, or yesterday — not as your only way to add food.

The daily loop

  1. 1
    Log on Home (Buddy)

    Text, camera, gallery, or mic. Buddy estimates nutrition, then saves a diary row.

  2. 2
    Confirm if Buddy asks

    Check name, amount, and numbers. One meal = one Foodiary row.

  3. 3
    Watch Today's Progress

    Energy, protein, fiber, water fill from what you logged. Tap a metric for details.

  4. 4
    Use Foodiary to edit

    Fix grams, time, leftovers — same diary Progress reads.
