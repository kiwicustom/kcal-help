How kcal works

One loop. Log food with Buddy on Home → it lands in your Foodiary → Today's Progress updates. That is the whole system for daily use.

Beta tip (Silke & friends): Prefer the big orange Log on Home (text / photo / voice). Open Foodiary to fix grams, times, or yesterday — not as your only way to add food.

The daily loop

  1. 1
    Log on Home (Buddy)

    Tap Log as Text, or use camera, gallery, or microphone. Buddy estimates calories, protein, fiber, and more, then saves a diary row.

  2. 2
    Confirm if Buddy asks

    Check the name, amount, and numbers. Confirm once. One meal = one Foodiary row.

  3. 3
    Watch Today's Progress

    Energy budget, protein, fiber, and water fill from what you logged. Tap any metric for details.

  4. 4
    Use Foodiary to edit

    Change grams, time, leftovers, or add a manual row when you already know the numbers. Same diary Buddy writes into.
