Numbered: what to do when

  1. 1
    Normal meal → Home Log

    Fastest path. Buddy does the nutrition work. Confirm once.

  2. 2
    Wrong grams or time → Foodiary

    Open the row, fix it. Do not re-log a duplicate on Home unless you deleted the old one.

  3. 3
    Yesterday / past day → pick the date, then Log or Foodiary

    Home date control + Foodiary day editor both care which day you mean.

  4. 4
    Only Foodiary, blank kcal → Progress stays empty

    Manual rows need kcal (typed or estimated from name). Prefer Buddy Log so estimates happen for you.

  5. 5
    Both update Today's Progress

    There is one nutrition store per day. Buddy and Foodiary are not two competing ledgers.

Keep both. We will not remove Foodiary. We teach Buddy-first so Progress fills without homework.
