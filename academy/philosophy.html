Foundation

kCal Philosophy

Helping people build healthier lives through knowledge, consistency and intelligent coaching.

Health is not a destination.
It is the result of thousands of small decisions made consistently over time.
kCal Buddy exists to make those decisions easier.

Our beliefs

Progress over Perfection

Small improvements repeated consistently beat short bursts of perfection.

Knowledge over Guessing

Understanding why creates lasting habits. Buddy should teach, not only count.

Consistency over Intensity

Healthy routines outperform unsustainable extremes.

Honesty over Shame

There are no bad days. Honest logs help Buddy coach better.

Protein Matters

Protein supports muscle, recovery and satiety.

Fibre Matters

Fibre supports digestion, fullness and long-term health.

Recovery Matters

Sleep, hydration and recovery are essential.

AI Should Coach

AI should reduce stress and empower — never judge.

Health Should Scale

Habits should spread to families, friends, workplaces, coaches and communities.
