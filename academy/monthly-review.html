Monthly Review

Why this matters

Months show direction. Days show noise. Zoom out monthly.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Compare this month's average adherence to last month.

  2. 2

    Update goals if life changed (travel, training block, stress).

  3. 3

    Archive wins: recipes that worked, logging modes you prefer (voice vs text).

  4. 4

    Set one theme for next month (e.g. protein at breakfast).

💬 Buddy Tip

If the scale stalled but logs are honest, check fiber, protein, sleep, and steps — not only kcal.

⚠
Common mistake

Quitting because one month was messy. Restart with a smaller target.
