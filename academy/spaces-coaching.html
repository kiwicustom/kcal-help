Spaces & Coaching

Why this matters

Together features keep you accountable — Buddy still logs your personal diary.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Open Spaces to see communities you belong to.

  2. 2

    Join or accept invites when you are ready to share context.

  3. 3

    Keep personal logging on Home — Spaces do not replace Buddy Log.

  4. 4

    If a coach/desk appears later, treat it as support on top of your diary — not a second app.

💬 Buddy Tip

Belonging helps adherence. Privacy: share Spaces deliberately.

⚠
Common mistake

Waiting for a perfect Space before logging personally. Log first; join when ready.
