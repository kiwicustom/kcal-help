FAQ

Why this matters

Fast answers to the questions that stall new users.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Progress at 0? Log on Home with kcal, same day, same account — then refresh.

  2. 2

    Foodiary vs Buddy? Buddy writes the diary; Foodiary edits it.

  3. 3

    Voice saved nothing? Confirm the proposal before leaving.

  4. 4

    Wrong day? Change the date on Home / Foodiary before logging.

  5. 5

    Need Help in-app? Menu → Help → help.kcal.lol.

💬 Buddy Tip

Still stuck? Feedback in the app or hello@kcal.lol — include what you tapped.

⚠
Common mistake

Assuming Foodiary is a separate calorie app. It is the same diary.
