First 30 Days

Why this matters

The first month builds the habit loop. Consistency beats perfection.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Aim to log most meals for 7 days straight — even imperfect estimates count.

  2. 2

    Weigh in on a steady schedule if weight is part of your goal (optional).

  3. 3

    Once a week, open Progress and notice trends — not day-to-day noise.

  4. 4

    Use Meal Plan lightly: plan 2–3 dinners you will actually cook.

💬 Buddy Tip

Missing one meal is fine. Missing the whole day twice in a row is the habit killer — restart at the next meal.

⚠
Common mistake

Waiting for a perfect week before starting. Start today with one honest log.
