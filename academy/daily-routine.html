Daily Routine

Why this matters

A tiny repeatable ritual beats motivation. Home is the ritual stage.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Morning: open Home, check remaining energy and water.

  2. 2

    Each meal: Log on Home (text/photo/voice) → confirm → done.

  3. 3

    Evening: skim Foodiary for leftovers or wrong times; fix one thing if needed.

  4. 4

    Optional: tap mood / quick water so the day feels complete.

💬 Buddy Tip

Same time window every day (e.g. after dinner) locks the habit.

⚠
Common mistake

Opening five tabs before logging. One orange button is enough.
