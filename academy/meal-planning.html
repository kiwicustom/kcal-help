Meal Planning

Why this matters

A light plan reduces decision fatigue and impulsive skips.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Open Meal Plan and sketch the next 2–3 days — not the whole month.

  2. 2

    Reuse meals you already logged well (Foodiary history helps).

  3. 3

    When you cook the plan, log on Home as usual (or mark eaten when that flow applies).

  4. 4

    Generate a shopping list from the plan when you use that feature.

💬 Buddy Tip

Plan dinners you will cook on busy nights. Leave lunch flexible if needed.

⚠
Common mistake

Planning seven gourmet dinners you will never make — then feeling behind.
