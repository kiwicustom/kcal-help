Fiber

Fibre supports digestion, fullness, blood sugar steadiness, and long-term health. It is a daily building block, not a punitive “detox” add-on.

⏱ 8 min Beginner
💬 Buddy Tip

Add fibre to meals you already eat — berries on yoghurt, beans in soup, veg on the protein plate — before buying specialty products.

🎯 Today's Mission

Include one fibre-rich plant food at lunch and dinner today; log them.

WHY?

Why this matters

Fibre-rich foods slow digestion, help you feel fuller on a given calorie budget, and feed a healthier gut environment. People who “eat enough protein” but still snack endlessly often lack fibre and volume from plants.

Sudden fibre spikes cause bloating. The coaching move is gradual increase with water — not a weekend of bran heroics after a low-fibre month.

Fibre is part of why equal calories are not equal outcomes. A calorie-matched meal with vegetables, legumes, and whole grains usually behaves differently than a low-fibre refined plate.

WHAT?

What to understand

Know the practical sources and the pacing.

  1. 1

    Vegetables, fruit, legumes, oats, whole grains, nuts, and seeds carry fibre

  2. 2

    Both soluble and insoluble fibre help — diversity beats one magic food

  3. 3

    Increase slowly if your baseline is low; pair with water

  4. 4

    Fibre supplements can help fill gaps but should not replace plants

  5. 5

    Track consistency of fibre-containing meals, not only a perfect gram number

Myth
Juice cleanses are a fibre strategy.

Truth: Juicing often removes fibre. Eat the fruit and vegetables when you can.

Myth
Low-carb means zero fibre.

Truth: Non-starchy vegetables, seeds, and some berries still fit many lower-carb patterns.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

Make fibre automatic in the weekly shop.

  1. 1

    Put two vegetables, one fruit, and one legume or whole-grain staple on every shopping list

  2. 2

    Add a plant side to your protein anchor at lunch and dinner

  3. 3

    Swap one refined grain for a higher-fibre option you actually enjoy

  4. 4

    Raise fibre over 1–2 weeks if your gut complains

  5. 5

    Review the week: how many days had obvious fibre sources at two meals?

How Buddy helps

Logging plants and legumes teaches Progress what “fibre days” look like. Buddy can coach plate balance when Foodiary shows protein without plants.

Fullness on a budget

High-fibre foods add chewing, volume, and slower digestion — useful when calories are limited. Pair with protein for the best satiety stack.

If evenings are snack-heavy, audit lunch fibre and protein before blaming willpower.

Comfort and travel

Travel often drops fibre. Pack portable options (fruit, veggie sticks, roasted legumes) or choose vegetable sides when eating out.

Return to baseline gradually after low-fibre trips to avoid GI drama.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I added 40g of fibre overnight with powder.”

    Rapid jumps cause discomfort and scare people off fibre. Titrate.

  • “Ketchup and a leaf of lettuce covered my vegetables.”

    Token plants do not deliver fibre. Aim for visible portions.

  • “Fibre makes me gain weight so I avoid it.”

    Early scale bumps can be gut content and water. Fat gain is not the usual story — give trends time.

❤️ Encouragement

Fibre is quiet health infrastructure. Keep adding plants you like until fullness feels easier — that is the win.
