Voice Logging

Why this matters

Voice removes friction when your hands are full — but confirm before save.

Step-by-step

    On Home, tap the mic / Talk food.

  2. 2

    Speak the meal clearly (food + amount if you know it).

  3. 3

    Wait for transcript and Buddy's proposal.

  4. 4

    Confirm once — one Foodiary row.
