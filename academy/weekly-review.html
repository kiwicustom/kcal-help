Weekly Review

Why this matters

Weekly review turns raw logs into learning without daily obsession.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Open Progress and look at the week — protein consistency and weight trend.

  2. 2

    Scan Foodiary for patterns (late dinners, skipped breakfast, low fiber days).

  3. 3

    Adjust next week's Meal Plan by one realistic change.

  4. 4

    Celebrate adherence, not a perfect number on the scale.

💬 Buddy Tip

Ask: What did I do on good days? Repeat that — not a crash diet.

⚠
Common mistake

Changing five variables at once after one heavy weekend.
