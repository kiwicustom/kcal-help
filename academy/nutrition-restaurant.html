Restaurant Eating

Restaurants are part of a normal life. The skill is honest logging and a few defaults — not avoiding every invitation.

⏱ 8 min Beginner
💬 Buddy Tip

Decide your protein order first, then build sides. Invisible oils are expected — estimate them instead of logging a fantasy grilled-only plate.

🎯 Today's Mission

At your next restaurant meal, pick a protein-forward main, enjoy it, and log it the same day with a fair fat estimate.

WHY?

Why this matters

Social meals sustain relationships — and relationships sustain habits. A plan that forbids restaurants usually fails the first birthday dinner.

Restaurant calories are uncertain. Coaching aims for directional honesty: protein source, rough carbs, and a non-zero fat assumption beat precise fiction.

One restaurant meal rarely breaks a week. A weekend of unlogged feasts plus Monday punishment might.

WHAT?

What to understand

Practical restaurant tactics.

  1. 1

    Scan the menu for a clear protein centre

  2. 2

    Ask for dressings and sauces on the side when it helps you

  3. 3

    Share large sides or box half if portions are huge

  4. 4

    Alcohol counts — log it

  5. 5

    Voice-log at the table or right after: “salmon, potatoes, butter sauce, wine”

Myth
If I can’t know exact grams, I shouldn’t log.

Truth: A fair estimate keeps Progress usable. Silence creates bigger errors.

Myth
Salad is always the low-calorie choice.

Truth: Cheese, candied nuts, and creamy dressing can exceed a simple hot protein plate.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

Use a short pre-game, then enjoy the people.

  1. 1

    Preview the menu if possible; choose a default protein dish category

  2. 2

    Eat a normal protein-aware day around the meal — don’t “save” all calories and arrive ravenous

  3. 3

    Order intentionally; skip the guilt narration at the table

  4. 4

    Log with Buddy (voice is ideal) including oils/sauces/drinks

  5. 5

    Return to your usual plan at the next meal — no compensatory starve

How Buddy helps

Voice and camera capture messy real-world meals quickly. Foodiary lets you refine later if you learn more about the dish.

Cuisine-specific defaults

Grill/fish houses: protein + potato + vegetable, watch butter sauces.

Pizza: add a protein side salad, decide slice count in advance, log oil-rich reality.

Buffets: one plate strategy — protein and plants first.

Business lunches

Default to water or one drink; keep afternoon work in mind. Logging afterward matters more than performing restriction in front of colleagues.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I logged grilled chicken breast with zero oil because I scraped the sauce.”

    Cooking fat remains. Add a modest oil estimate.

  • “I skipped logging so I wouldn’t see the damage.”

    Avoidance blocks learning. Honest ugly logs are brave and useful.

  • “I punished yesterday’s dinner with no food until 6pm.”

    Compensation increases the next overeat. Resume normal structure.

❤️ Encouragement

Belonging and food can coexist. Order like an adult, log like a scientist, and rejoin your routine at the next meal.
