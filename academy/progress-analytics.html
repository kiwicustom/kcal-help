Progress & Analytics

Why this matters

Progress is the feedback loop that keeps the habit honest.

Step-by-step

  1. 1

    Use Today's Progress on Home for the day.

  2. 2

    Use the Progress tab for trends (weight, protein consistency).

  3. 3

    Tap metric cards for details and sources.

  4. 4

    If numbers look wrong, fix Foodiary rows for that day — don't invent a second log.

💬 Buddy Tip

Empty Progress almost always means empty or kcal-less diary rows for that day.

⚠
Common mistake

Treating one spike day as failure. Look at the week.
