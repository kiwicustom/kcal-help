Home

Home is your daily desk. Buddy greets you, then his toolbox logs the day. Today’s Progress fills after you save. Beginner, Standard, and Power User are the same Home with more or less on screen — switch with PX in the bottom bar.

⏱ 7 min Beginner

Your Home screen (mobile)

Home is the daily desk. Same data in every Experience Mode — only how much you see changes. Tap the ? in the top bar to label Home in waves. On-screen Click here to CONTINUE / Click here to go BACK, and Click here to CLOSE on the last wave — nothing auto-advances. Tap PX in the bottom bar to try Beginner, Standard, or Power User. No data loss.

🎯 Today's Mission

Open Home, tap Camera (or Water / Weight), confirm Buddy’s estimate, and watch Today’s Progress (or your Beginner task list) update after save.

WHY?

Why this matters

Wrong-door logging is the most common frustration. People open Foodiary to invent a blank row, leave kcal empty, then wonder why Progress stays at zero. Home is the smart door into the ledger.

The orange Camera button is Buddy’s main food log. Water, Weight, and Cart sit beside it. Text input is a Camera method, not a second Food button.

Experience Mode (PX) is presentation only. Beginner hides the dashboard. Standard shows the budget. Power adds TEF and leucine. Your logs do not change.

WHAT?

What to understand

What you should recognise on Home (mobile).

  1. 1

    Buddy host — greeting plus Camera, Water, Weight, Cart

  2. 2

    Camera — tap to scan a pack or plate; chevron chooses Camera, Photo, Receipt / File, Text input, Voice

  3. 3

    Water · Weight · Cart — run immediately

  4. 4

    Today’s Progress (Standard / Power) or Today’s Health + task list (Beginner)

  5. 5

    PX pill in the bottom bar — Beg / Std / Pwr

  6. 6

    Bottom tabs — Beginner is Home · Foodiary · Progress · Menu; Standard and Power add Meal Plan, Activity, Spaces

Myth
Home is just a dashboard; real logging happens in Foodiary.

Truth: Prefer Home for new meals. Foodiary is for review and corrections.

Myth
Tapping Camera only chooses a method.

Truth: Camera logs immediately with the selected method. The chevron only chooses the method.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

Make Home the reflex.

  1. 1

    Open the app to Home

  2. 2

    Tap Camera to scan, or pick Text input from the Camera chevron to type

  3. 3

    Confirm Buddy’s estimate; adjust grams if you know better

  4. 4

    Use Water, Weight, or Cart when that is the job

  5. 5

    Glance at Today’s Progress or the task list — then return to life

How Buddy helps

Buddy reduces friction and teaches while capturing. Progress on Home is immediate feedback that the loop worked.

Experience Mode (PX)

Beginner: greeting, today’s health, daily task list. Tap a meal row to log. No camera bar, no Meal Plan / Activity / Spaces tabs.

Standard: log bar + energy budget (Food · Exercise · Remaining) + health cards.

Power User: same as Standard, plus TEF on the budget and leucine on protein.

PX is beta presentation. No data loss when you switch.

Today’s Progress

Treat it as a compass, not a courtroom. If it doesn’t move, check that the saved row has energy values and that you’re viewing today.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I added a Foodiary row with no kcal and thought Home was broken.”

    Blank-kcal rows don’t teach Progress. Log via Buddy on Home or fill nutrition.

  • “I looked for a Food button next to Camera.”

    Food logging is Camera. Type a meal with Text input in the Camera menu. Weight is the fourth chip.

  • “I only open Progress tab, never Home.”

    Progress needs inputs. Capture on Home first.
