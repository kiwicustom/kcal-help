Help

Spaces

Spaces are private invitation-only containers — family, friends, teams, coaches improving together. Buddy still coaches you. Your Foodiary stays yours.

⏱ 6 min Beginner
💬 Buddy Tip

Nothing is shared automatically. Friends see that you showed up — never your calories, weight, or Foodiary.

🎯 Today's Mission

Open Spaces (on spaces.kcal.lol), open a Space you belong to, and walk Home · Wall · Challenges · Progress · People. Settings is the gear.

WHY?

Why this matters

Buddy helps you. Spaces help us. Coach Desk (later) helps many. Together that is the Health Operating System.

You never browse, search, or discover Spaces. You only see Spaces you belong to. Invitation-only.

Privacy is sacred. Share participation if you want. Calories, macros, weight, and Foodiary stay private.

WHAT?

What to understand

Five screens. One question each. Same in every Space.

  1. 1

    Home — What are WE doing today? Who’s here, Buddy, the challenge, Open Foodiary.

  2. 2

    Wall — What happened today? Signal, not chat.

  3. 3

    Challenges — What are we trying to achieve? Current, upcoming (coming soon), history.

  4. 4

    Progress — How are WE improving? Consistency and show-up — never a calorie scoreboard.

  5. 5

    People — Who is doing this together? Encourage with one tap.

  6. 6

    Settings (gear) — What do I want to share? Invite lives here too.

Myth
Spaces means public social media for my calories.

Truth: Spaces are intentional groups. You control context and membership. Calories never appear.

Myth
If I’m in a Space, I must be perfect for others.

Truth: Honesty over Shame still applies. Real peers prefer truth to theatre.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

Start with people you would invite in real life.

  1. 1

    On spaces.kcal.lol, open Menu → Spaces

  2. 2

    Create a Space or open an invite that says we want you with us

  3. 3

    Invite only people you actually know — nobody browses in

  4. 4

    Show up in Foodiary. Presence returns to the Space

  5. 5

    Encourage a quiet friend from Home or People

  6. 6

    Leave from Settings if a Space stops feeling safe

How Buddy helps

Buddy remains your personal coach. Spaces add belonging. Together they support the Health Operating System’s social layer.

Family Spaces

People first. Encourage each other. Never compete on weight or calories at home.

Protect kids’ dignity — Spaces are not a venue for public calorie policing.

Coach-supported Spaces

Coaches support the group. They do not own your Foodiary. Share participation; keep private logs under your control.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I inflated my logs so the group would be impressed.”

    That breaks your Progress and the group’s trust. Honesty scales better.

  • “I joined ten Spaces and engaged with none.”

    Belonging needs focus. One active Space beats ten badges.

  • “Someone shamed my weekend — so I stopped logging.”

    Wrong culture. Address norms or leave. Don’t abandon your diary.

❤️ Encouragement

Health should spread. Start with people who make showing up easier — that is already Scale Health Success.
