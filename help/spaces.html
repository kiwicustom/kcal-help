WHAT?

What a Space is

A Space is a small community with a shared challenge window (for example September Spartan): name, dates, shared goal, members, and a calm “who showed up today” signal.

kcal.lol platform layers you will hear:

  • Buddy — your personal coach and Foodiary (intelligence).
  • Spaces — collaboration with your people (this guide).
  • Desks — professional tools later (Coach Desk is not live yet).

You never need the word “Workspace”. That is an internal engineering term only.

WHERE?

Where to open Spaces

Use the Spaces host:

https://spaces.kcal.lol

  • You need a spaces.kcal.lol invite key. Keys from beta.kcal.lol / app.kcal.lol do not work here (separate database).
  • On personal beta, Spaces is turned off on purpose. Collaboration lives on the Spaces host during Together Era validation.

Every screen should answer: Who is here? What are we doing today? What should I do next? Who needs encouragement? What have we achieved together?

SCREEN

Spaces list

What you see: kcal.lol branding, big “Spaces” title, tagline Same journey. Stronger together., orange + Create a new Space, then a card per Space with a warm hero image, name, day progress, face placeholders, mission hint.

Good for: picking which crew to open, or starting a new challenge.

⋯ on a card / long-press: opens Space options (invite, edit, leave, delete) without entering the Space.

SCREEN

Create Space

What you set:

  1. 1

    Name — e.g. September Spartan

  2. 2

    Shared goal — Honest daily logging · Morning weight · Finish the day · or Custom

  3. 3

    Start & end dates — default about 30 days

After create: Buddy congratulates you and you can Share invite immediately. Do that — an empty Space is just a folder.

SCREEN

Invite & join

Invite link

Looks like https://spaces.kcal.lol/#/join-space/…. Share via Messages, WhatsApp, Mail — whatever your friends actually use.

Join landing

Friend sees Space name, who invited them, dates, goal, and a short privacy summary before joining.

Join Space

Signed-in friend confirms consent and joins the crew. They land on Space Home.

Privacy

Friends see that you are in the Space and whether you showed up today. They do not see your meals, calories, or weight numbers.

SCREEN

Space Home — every section

This is the daily ritual. Order matters. People come before numbers.

  1. 1

    Tabs — Home · Team · Moments · Goals

  2. 2

    Complete banner (only when finished) → Celebrate

  3. 3

    Micro-delight — occasional warm one-liner

  4. 4

    Hero — sunrise artwork, Space name, Day X of N

  5. 5

    Your people — faces with Logged / You’re next / Not yet

  6. 6

    Buddy — who showed up, who’s last, full-crew cheer

  7. 7

    Today’s mission — one line (e.g. Log honestly) + big Open Foodiary

  8. 8

    Team progress this week — one emotional bar + sentence (not a metric dashboard)

  9. 9

    Recent wins — up to five celebrate lines

  10. 10

    Invite (owner) — More friends, more good days · Share Invite

  11. 11

    ⋯ — options sheet

Good for: opening the Space every day, seeing your people, doing one honest action, inviting when ready.

The only mission button is Open Foodiary. Logging on Buddy is how you “show up” for most goals.

TABS

Team · Moments · Goals

Team

What: roster with large faces, Logged today / streak / Not yet, Buddy “Stronger together”, Invite for owners.

Good for: seeing who’s in and who might need a gentle nudge. Never calories or weight shame.

Moments

What: chat-style bubbles of wins and milestones, plus a live Buddy nudge.

Good for: light celebration. This is not WhatsApp — there is no free-form group chat box.

Goals

What: shared journey hero, team progress bar, goal rows, who showed up, Open Foodiary again.

Good for: remembering what WE are aiming at together — not a personal macro cockpit.

SCREEN

Challenge complete

When the end date passes, Home offers Celebrate. You see days together, Buddy’s line, and (if you own the Space) Start next challenge — for example October Spartan.

Good for: finishing with pride and immediately starting the next chapter together.

SHEET

⋯ Actions sheet

  • Jump to Goals / Team / Moments
  • Invite a Friend (owner)
  • Edit Space (owner, while active)
  • Leave Space (members; owners may transfer ownership first)
  • Delete Space for everyone (owner confirm)
HOW?

How “Logged” / showed up works

Shared goalYou show up when…
Honest daily loggingYou log at least one Foodiary item today
Finish the daySame — Foodiary activity today
Morning weightYou record morning weight today
CustomManual check-in (no automatic diary signal)

Friends never see what you ate or your numbers — only that you participated.

NOT

What Spaces are not

  • Not Instagram / Facebook for calories
  • Not a chat app (Moments ≠ messaging)
  • Not Coach Desk (professional console comes later)
  • Not nested team hierarchies yet
  • Not a place to shame weekend logs — leave if culture turns toxic
⚠ Mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I faked logs so the group would be impressed.”

    That breaks Buddy’s Method and the crew’s trust. Honesty scales.

  • “I created a Space and never invited anyone.”

    Invite is part of the product. Empty Spaces do not validate Together Era.

  • “I joined ten Spaces and engaged with none.”

    One active crew beats ten badges.

❤️ Encouragement

Health is better together. Open your Space, see your people, log honestly, cheer the last Spartan.
