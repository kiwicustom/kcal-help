Help

Health Integrations

Health Integrations is where you connect Apple Health (and see what’s coming for Garmin, Withings, Oura, and more). Buddy stays the coach — Apple Health stays the hub. You own the data.

⏱ 5 min Beginner
💬 Buddy Tip

Apple Health Connect and Sync now run in the kcal iOS app (TestFlight). Safari cannot talk to HealthKit. After Connect, tap Sync now for the last 14 days.

🎯 Today's Mission

On iPhone, open Connected Health / Health Sources, tap Connect on Apple Health, grant HealthKit, then Sync now.

WHY?

Why this matters

One place for health sources beats hunting through Menu and Connected Health separately.

Every permission explains why Buddy wants it — no blind “Allow all”.

Disconnect anytime. Sync now pulls HealthKit samples into Buddy — it does not happen from Safari.

Coming-soon cards (Garmin, Withings, WHOOP, Fitbit, Health Connect) stay Later until those bridges ship.

WHAT?

What to understand

What you will see.

  1. 1

    Settings → Health Integrations — open the page

  2. 2

    Apple Health card — Connect when you are ready

  3. 3

    Permission list — Weight, Sleep, Heart Rate, and more, each with a short why

  4. 4

    Connected dashboard — status, permissions granted, data sources (foundation)

  5. 5

    Coming soon — Garmin, Withings, Oura, Strava, Fitbit, Android Health Connect

  6. 6

    Buddy placeholders — sleep / recovery / heart trends (not coaching yet)

Myth
Connecting Apple Health on a Mac already syncs sleep and workouts into Buddy.

Truth: Connect and Sync now need the kcal iOS app. Safari / Mac cannot read HealthKit.

Myth
I can finish the real HealthKit grant on a MacBook alone.

Truth: You can review the screens on beta web. Real Apple Health / HealthKit needs an iPhone.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

How to set it up (beta).

  1. 1

    Update to the latest beta (web hard-refresh, or newest TestFlight).

  2. 2

    Open Menu → Connected Health (Health Sources).

  3. 3

    On iPhone TestFlight, tap Connect on Apple Health and grant HealthKit.

  4. 4

    Tap Sync now for the last 14 days (weight, sleep, workouts, steps, heart, active kcal).

  5. 5

    Return to Home and keep logging meals — Health Integrations supports Buddy; it does not replace Foodiary.

How Buddy helps

Buddy will coach from health context in later phases. Today, honest logging still teaches the most.

Where to find it

Path: Menu → Settings → Health Integrations → Open Health Integrations.

Direct path on your invite URL: …/#/u/YOUR_KEY/health-integrations (same page).

Permissions Buddy may ask for

Weight, body fat, lean mass, steps, walking distance, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, workouts, active energy, exercise minutes, VO2 max, hydration, nutrition, blood pressure, blood oxygen.

You choose which ones to grant. We do not request “everything” silently.

Privacy

Health data never leaves the device without your explicit consent.

Phase 1 foundation connection is local. No dark patterns — disconnect anytime.

Existing Connected Health (Oura / Google / Strava OAuth) remains available under Connected Health for live cloud sources.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I connected Apple Health and expected sleep coaching the same day.”

    Coaching and auto-sync are later phases. This release ships the foundation screen.

  • “I skipped the why text and allowed everything.”

    Read the reasons. Granular choice is the point.
