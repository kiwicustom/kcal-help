Help

Progress

Progress turns honest logs into trends. Days are noisy; weeks and months show direction — protein, weight, and adherence included.

⏱ 8 min Beginner
💬 Buddy Tip

Open Progress weekly with one question: “What did good days share?” Change one variable afterward.

🎯 Today's Mission

Open Progress, scan the last week, and write one keep / one change note.

WHY?

Why this matters

Without Progress, Foodiary is a pile of receipts. Trends reveal whether your energy budget, protein anchors, and habits are working under real life.

Daily obsession with a single weigh-in creates false emergencies. Progress exists to zoom out so you can coach calmly.

Progress only works if logs are honest and complete enough. Capture on Home; correct in Foodiary; learn here.

Progress is also where you notice skill gains: fewer blank days, better restaurant estimates, steadier protein. Those wins deserve equal attention beside the scale.

Myth
Progress at 0 means the app is broken.

Truth: Often missing kcal on rows, wrong day selected, or logging only in empty Foodiary placeholders. Check the Quick Guide path.

Myth
I should change five targets every time Progress looks messy.

Truth: Multi-change weeks teach nothing. Adjust one lever.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

Make Progress a weekly appointment.

  1. 1

    Ensure the week has enough Home logs with nutrition

  2. 2

    Open Progress; note direction, not drama

  3. 3

    Tap into odd days via Foodiary context

  4. 4

    Celebrate adherence before criticising averages

  5. 5

    Set one theme for next week

  6. 6

    Return to daily ritual — don’t live inside charts

How Buddy helps

Buddy capture feeds Progress. Coaching quality rises when trends reflect real restaurants, travel, and training — not curated highlights.

Reading weight

Prefer multi-day or weekly trends. Sodium, carbs, and hormones move water. Fat change is slower.

Reading protein

Count how often anchors appeared. Two heroic protein days don’t cancel five low ones for how the week felt.

Monthly zoom

Once a month, widen the window. Ask whether goals still fit life, then change at most one target. Archive a win in writing so memory does not erase it.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I judged my whole program on this morning’s weigh-in.”

    Use Progress trends. One morning is weather, not climate.

  • “Progress looked bad so I deleted old logs.”

    Deleting erases learning. Keep honesty; adjust forward.

  • “I only check Progress when I feel guilty.”

    Scheduled calm reviews beat shame-triggered chart spirals.

❤️ Encouragement

Progress is a mirror for patterns, not a verdict on your worth. Log honestly, review weekly, improve once — repeat.
