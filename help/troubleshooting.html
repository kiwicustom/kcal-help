Help

Troubleshooting

Most “bugs” are path or day mismatches: logged in the wrong place, viewed the wrong day, or saved rows without kcal. Walk the checklist before you lose a week of patience.

⏱ 9 min Beginner
💬 Buddy Tip

Reproduce once on Home with a simple meal that has clear kcal. If Progress moves, the system works — fix the path, not your motivation.

🎯 Today's Mission

If something feels broken, run the three checks: Home log path, selected day, kcal on the row.

WHY?

Why this matters

Troubleshooting skill protects Consistency over Intensity. People quit when they think the product ignored their effort — often the effort landed in an empty row or another date.

Sync delays, offline moments, and confirm-skipped voice drafts also confuse. A short ritual finds the cause without shame.

When it’s truly stuck, hello@kcal.lol helps faster if you can say what you tried: device, day, whether Home or Foodiary, and a screenshot if possible.

WHAT?

What to understand

Highest-frequency issues.

  1. 1

    Progress at 0 — blank kcal, placeholders, or no Home log

  2. 2

    Wrong day — late logs / selected date mismatch

  3. 3

    Voice/camera draft not saved — confirm skipped

  4. 4

    Meal Plan vs diary confusion — intent logged as intake (or intake never logged)

  5. 5

    Sync / reinstall surprises — auth and recovery gaps

Myth
If Progress is 0, my data is gone.

Truth: Often the data is in Foodiary on another day or without energy fields. Inspect before panicking.

Myth
Reinstalling always fixes it.

Truth: Reinstall without a clear auth/recovery plan can make things worse. Diagnose first.

HOW?

How to do this in real life

Use this order every time.

  1. 1

    Log one known meal on Home with Buddy; confirm save

  2. 2

    Open Foodiary for today — see the row and its kcal/protein

  3. 3

    Open Progress — confirm it responds

  4. 4

    If empty: check selected day; fix blank-kcal rows; avoid duplicate manual empties

  5. 5

    For voice/camera: retry and explicitly confirm

  6. 6

    Still stuck: note device + time + what you tapped; email hello@kcal.lol

How Buddy helps

The Quick Guide (WHAT / WHY / HOW) matches this path. Buddy capture with nutrition is the fastest proof the loop is alive.

Progress at 0

Blank-kcal Foodiary rows do not feed Progress. Prefer Buddy logs from Home. Edit rows to include energy or delete useless placeholders.

  1. 1

    Home → Log → confirm

  2. 2

    Foodiary → verify numbers

  3. 3

    Progress → refresh view / revisit today

Wrong day

Check the date control in Foodiary. Late-night entries may sit on yesterday or tomorrow relative to your mental model. Align the day you’re judging in Progress.

Sync and auth

Confirm you’re on a trusted/paired device and online long enough to sync. If you switched phones, finish pairing before declaring data missing.

⚠ Common mistakes

Common mistakes

  • “I added five empty Foodiary lines to “force” Progress.”

    Empty lines add noise. One complete Buddy log teaches more.

  • “I emailed support with only “it doesn’t work.””

    Path, day, and screenshot cut resolution time dramatically.

  • “I stopped logging for a week until it’s fixed.”

    Try the Home proof log first. Often you’re unblocked in two minutes — and gaps hurt learning.

❤️ Encouragement

You are not failing the app. Walk Home → Foodiary → Progress once with a real meal. Most mysteries end there — and Buddy will be waiting at the next log.
