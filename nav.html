Bottom tabs

Seven places. One job each. Start on Home for logging.

  1. 1
    Home

    Buddy Log + Today's Progress + mood. Daily default.

  2. 2
    Meal Plan

    What you plan to eat across days — planning, not the live diary.

  3. 3
    Foodiary

    The day book of what you already ate. Edit and review here.

  4. 4
    Activity

    Movement, burn, activity calendar views.

  5. 5
    Progress

    Longer-term charts and trends — not the same as Today's Progress on Home.

  6. 6
    Spaces

    Together / community spaces (shared context with other people).

  7. 7
    Menu

    Overflow: About, Privacy, Terms, Get the App, Help, feedback, extras.
